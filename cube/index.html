Rules

Swipe to roll the cube one tile. The face that lands underneath stamps its colour on the tile, over whatever was there.

A tile with a coloured rim asks for that colour. Fill every rim with its own colour and the level is done. Tiles without a rim can be any colour.

The face on the side you roll towards is the one that goes under. The small picture shows all six faces: roll right and the face on its right is stamped.

The way you came decides how the cube sits. A lap round four tiles turns it, which is how you bring another colour within reach.

There is no clock. Par is the fewest rolls a level can be done in.
