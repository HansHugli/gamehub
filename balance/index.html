Balance

New puzzle

Rules

Fill every cell with a circle or a square. Tap a cell to change it: circle, square, empty.

  1. Never three alike in a line, across or down.
  2. Every row and every column holds as many circles as squares.
  3. No two rows are the same, and no two columns.

The cells with a darker background are given and stay as they are. Every puzzle has one answer and never needs a guess.
